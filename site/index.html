EE Labs

Interactive labs for the signals half of an electrical engineering curriculum. Every claim on every page is measured against what is actually drawn — the explanations are tested.

Signal Lab

Signals and DSP. Build waveforms from harmonics, sample and alias them, run them through filter chains — biquads and FIRs — and read the spectrum, the kernel, the z-plane, and the group delay of what you built.

28 guided presets →

Circuit Lab

RC, RLC, op-amp networks. Component values become poles and zeros, Bode plots and step responses — and every schematic's transfer function is derived where you can check it.

12 lessons, 9 circuits →

Control Lab

Close a loop. Six plants, four controllers, and the point −1: step responses, stability margins, root locus and Nyquist — with the rules of thumb stated as rules of thumb.

11 lessons, six plants →

The three labs share one spine. A series RLC in Circuit Lab is a biquad — hand it to Signal Lab and watch the same f₀ and Q filter a signal. The same network is a second-order plant — hand it to Control Lab and ask how much gain the loop takes before it sings. Not three approximations of each other: the same transfer function, read three ways.
